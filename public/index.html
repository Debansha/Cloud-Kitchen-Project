🧠 OS CPU Scheduling Performance Benchmarks

Educational Demo for Teacher / Viva

Why are OS Scheduling Algorithms used here? In a Cloud Kitchen, food orders arrive like CPU Processes, cooking stations act as CPU Cores, and food prep times represent Burst Times. Traditional kitchens use First-Come-First-Served (FCFS), which causes severe Convoy Effects (short orders stuck behind long pizza orders). Our system implements Shortest Job First (SJF) to minimize average wait time, and Priority Scheduling with Aging (LST algorithm, +3 pts/min) to ensure urgent orders never miss deadlines without starvation.
